HOBBY BRACKET 64

Four players, 16 hobbies each, 64 total. You get 100 points to spread across everyone else's hobbies (you can't rate your own). Pick your name to start.

How it works

  1. Each person clicks their name and assigns points (100 total) to the other 48 hobbies.
  2. Hit Finish & get my code and send that code to whoever is running the bracket.
  3. On Results & Bracket, paste all four codes and the 64-hobby bracket auto-fills — highest score wins each matchup.
